## Current coverage

The app is free. Its interface is currently in Simplified Chinese and its bundled dataset includes only Longyue Jingxu (龙樾璟序) in Shijingshan, Beijing, China. It does not include U.S. properties or worldwide property search. Requires iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Calculations use December 21, 2025 and June 21, 2026; displayed times use Beijing time (UTC+8).

## Getting started

Tap 龙樾璟序 on the home screen. Select a building and floor, then drag the time slider directly below the model. Use the play button to watch changes through the day. Switch between 冬至 (winter solstice) and 夏至 (summer solstice) for sunlight durations, intervals and blocking buildings. Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom or tap a building to select it.

## Building 18 sections

Building 18 has a west section with a maximum of 9 floors and an east section with a maximum of 11 floors. Switching sections automatically limits your floor selection. Buildings 9 and 17 are ancillary buildings.

## Opening a shared link

The shared text summary can be read directly. A guangjitanlou:// link requires the recipient to have the app installed. This version does not provide a browser-based page for individual analysis results.

## Removing local selections

Deleting the app normally removes saved local selections; iOS offloading may keep them. Backup restoration follows your system settings. The app has no sign-in accounts or server account data.

## Understanding estimates

Results estimate sunlight on a south-facing facade using a simplified model and are for understanding and comparison only. Buildings outside the property boundary and some architectural details are omitted. Rendered shadows are illustrative. Results are not a window-specific daylight assessment, planning compliance determination or property guarantee. Open 采光是怎么算的 in the app for calculation details.
