Scope and contact

This policy describes data handling for version 1.0.0 of the 光迹探楼 (Guang Ji Tan Lou) iOS app, this support website and support email. Contact the app developer at zusk66@gmail.com for privacy questions. This is a draft prepared for the first App Store release and will be checked against the final build before release.

Data handled by the app

No registration or login is required. We do not collect or upload personal information through the app. This version contains no advertising, behavioral analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information or track you across apps. Sunlight calculations run on your device using bundled property data and do not use your live location.

Locally saved selections

The app uses iOS local preferences to save your selected building, building section, floor and solstice date. We do not send these selections to a server. Selections are updated when you change them. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it while keeping its data, normally removes its local data. Device backup and restoration are controlled by Apple and your system settings.

Sharing and the clipboard

When you actively use the system share sheet and choose a destination, the analysis summary and app link are passed to the service you select. A summary may include the property, building, floor, reference date and estimated results. Tapping the button to copy the official drawing-source link writes that link to the system clipboard. The app does not read other clipboard content. Your selected sharing service handles data under its own policy.

External links and this website

Opening the official drawing-source link opens the system browser. External sites may process IP addresses and other access information under their own policies. This privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, tracking cookies or third-party fonts to these pages. GitHub may process visitor logs, including IP addresses, to provide and protect its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement: GitHub Privacy Statement . Core sunlight calculations do not depend on these webpages.

Support email

If you email us, we process the email address, message and any screenshots or other information you voluntarily provide to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled through Gmail. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or unrelated sensitive information. Support messages are normally retained for up to 12 months after resolution, then deleted or de-identified, unless retention is required by law. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion. We will verify and handle requests within applicable legal time limits.

System services and permissions

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone or the photo library. Apple device backups, system diagnostics and your chosen sharing services are controlled by their providers and your settings. We do not use them to track users within the app.

Children

The app is not specifically directed at children and does not collect children’s personal information through the app. Contact us if a support email contains information about a child that should not be retained.

Changes

We will update the date and content of this page if functionality or data handling changes and provide additional notice through the app or release information where necessary. Before the first release, refer to the final policy checked against the shipping build.
